## CLAIM A (Theorem 3: finite completion bound across repeated recoveries)
Closest: Enwerem 2610.07674 (progress fn + slack budget + expected-time bound; assumes recursive feasibility); Borquez 2312.15347 and 2609.17430 (single/hybrid liveness by BRT); van Wijk 2610.17337 (recurrence, no count); Dual-gatekeeper 2604.15507 (cumulative exploration budget, full state); Kim review 2604.02401 (stop-safe backups conservative).
Verdict: PARTIALLY COVERED. The "sum of progress is bounded by V0 plus a finite recovery budget" skeleton is Enwerem's Thm 1 (and a textbook ranking-function / multiple-Lyapunov argument). What is not found anywhere: (i) the bound derived under hard worst-case set-valued belief, (ii) recursive feasibility DISCHARGED by a stored non-anticipative backup into a belief-dependent task-ready set instead of assumed, (iii) occlusion-driven state-set growth as a recovery trigger inside the count.
SURVIVING WORDING: "In a backup-filter whose backup terminates in a belief-dependent task-ready set (not a stopped safe set), the closed loop, under worst-case set-valued parameter and state uncertainty, completes in at most V0/(delta - L_V r) nominal-recovery cycles; recursive feasibility is a consequence of the admission rule, not an assumption. Prior filters certify invariance (backup CBF/gatekeeper), single-event reach (HJ liveness), recurrence (2610.17337), or expected convergence assuming feasibility (Enwerem)."
PROOF-RISK TO FIX BEFORE CLAIMING: if occlusion makes sets grow DURING a nominal segment, V (belief-dependent) can rise. The count only holds if delta is net of worst-case growth over the admitted segment and re-observe returns V to <= its pre-occlusion value plus L_V r. Otherwise an adversarial occluder gives livelock. State a bounded-growth-rate assumption explicitly.
STRONGEST ATTACK: "This is Enwerem's Lyapunov-with-slack supermartingale, made deterministic; the bound V0/(delta-L_V r) is arithmetic and all the content hides in the assumption that a delta-progress step exists from every task-ready set (their Assumption 2 renamed)."

## Overall RSS-worthiness
A+B+C as a theory contribution: BORDERLINE. A is the headline but its mathematics is thin and Enwerem (Oct 2026, anchor) is a near-parallel with a stronger probabilistic frame; the defensible delta is hard set-valued belief + feasibility derived rather than assumed + occlusion/re-observe in the count. B is the most distinctive but only if it yields real sufficient conditions with a tight retreat counterexample. C alone is not RSS-level. Expect reviewers to ask "what does the guarantee buy over gatekeeper/HJ in practice."
Three things that strengthen it most:
1. Show the gap empirically and by construction: a minimal example (and sim tasks) where stop-safe backup filters (backup CBF/MPS/gatekeeper) and single-shot HJ liveness are safe but livelock or stall (infinite or very many recoveries), while task-ready admission completes within the Thm 3 bound; report measured cycle counts vs the bound (tightness).
2. Make Thm 3 non-trivial: prove recursive feasibility from the admission rule, handle bounded occlusion growth explicitly, and give a lower-bound/counterexample showing the delta - L_V r condition cannot be dropped.
3. Make Thm 4 checkable: an algorithmic certificate (margins on the null-space fibre + polytope-certified one-hand hold) with measured coverage on the sim task distribution, and a clean characterization of the forced-retreat cases.
Before submission: read 2609.17430 full text (Borquez hybrid) and check ARMOUR/WAITR fail-safe claim; both could narrow Claim A/B.
