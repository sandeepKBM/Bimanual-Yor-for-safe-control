## E4 library sweep (sweep_E4, 20 seeds, same-seed baselines)
With the library, results are IDENTICAL to without it:
- hold_mon: 5/20.
- set_mon: 11/20 (23.8 s).
- tracker mon_int: 0/20.
- 0 spills in every config.

Recoveries: direct 0, move 0, look 0, none 2789. The cause: the library is per-episode and filled only with the episode's own commits, so
no entry's precondition contains a stalled belief (a person standing close). It also adds lib.add ≈ 24 ms per commit.

Fixes:
1. A PERSISTENT library accumulated across episodes and seeds. This is the user's idea taken literally: keep building the set across runs.
2. Seeding with entries certified under varied people contexts.
3. A LOOK trigger that fires on untracked-near people, not only on out-of-FOV tracks.

## Open risks
1. **Progress:** the safe gatekeeper completes 25–55% vs the shield's 100%. The shield passes people at 0.18 m and inside the tracker's belief,
   so it is not certified. T3 says no bound exists without a fairness/liveness assumption, and the corridor geometry hits that.
2. **A1 coverage:** with the tracker, a person is untracked near the robot for long stretches. T1 only holds where A1 holds, so LOOK or
   sensor coverage is required.
3. **Latency:** candidate-set steps reach 107 ms (budget 20 ms). Needs a budget plus batched or GPU certification.
4. **Assumption sensitivity:**
   - C3 depends heavily on the CoM box (5% → 10% drops mugs from 59/59 to 10/59).
   - The probe σ/costs, e_v, a_p and the 20 N jaw force are assumptions.
5. **Conformal sets** are 95% sets. Intersecting them over time lowers coverage (union bound). The audit counter tracks misses.
6. **Uncommitted work:** everything above is uncommitted; nothing is pushed.
