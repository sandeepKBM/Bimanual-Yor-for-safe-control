What changed vs run158 (USER-approved 11-01; nothing else):
1. New ASSUMED bound CLAW_REL = 2 mm / 2 deg: the two claws keep their relative pose (right claw in the left claw frame: jaw-point
   offset and rotation) during the lift. The hold model treats both claws as one rigid fixture, so this was an implicit assumption;
   it is now stated and checked from the sim every step. A lift that breaks it is "assumption violated", not a soundness test.
   (Observable on the robot from joint encoders + FK, so a runtime monitor can enforce it later.)
2. Lid metric: sim fail on the lid = lid motion RELATIVE TO THE PAN during the lift > 5 mm (lid_lift_mm, measured from the lid's pose
   in the pan frame at the lift start). The clamp's earlier lid shove is recorded separately (lid_pre_mm) and reported, not scored as
   a lift failure. The old absolute metric (lid_mm) is still stored. Rotation of the lid vs the pan (lid_lift_deg) is diagnostic only.
3. Same worlds (segcert.worlds full 62 + iid 70 + overload 26), same chain (v1 start/pinch/push/clamp from the zed estimate), same
   verdicts (belief / truth at tilt 3 and 11 deg), same other thresholds (slip 2 mm / 3 deg per claw, ASSUMED), same seeds.

Primary metric: UNSOUND = admit & sim fail & every recorded assumption held (incl. CLAW_REL). Target 0.

lift); the 21 v2 counterexamples should move to "assumption violated" (right claw rotates ~10 deg). If any truth_t11 counterexample
