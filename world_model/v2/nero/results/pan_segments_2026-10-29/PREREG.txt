Segment-wise admission on the covered pan (user rule 10-29).  Written before the runs.  SIMULATION ONLY.

Code: world_model/v2/nero/pan/segments.py, segcert.py (branch feat/pan-2026-10-29, on top of 85be2c0).
Plan: push 0.07, pinch frac 0.65, grip 10 N, clamp geometry v2 (v1 = under_rim 10-28: right elbow on the table in every world).
Policy: observe_pose built (zed noise, 3 images then refuse), claws re-aimed at the estimate; path-aware pre-grasps (clearance >= 5 mm,
joint margin left 0.05 / right 0.01 rad, else refuse at B0); observable boundary checks after pinch/push/clamp; lift slip monitor.
Bounds: segments.BOUNDS (U, R, ROA, MON) -- ASSUMED unless marked MODEL; copied into every raw record.

Worlds (the same theta lists for every method):
  full     = 32 vertices + 30 i.i.d. interior of the prior box (= results/pan_fair_box_2026-10-29), +-1.5 cm, +-8 deg, mu x0.6-1.2, 0-0.4 kg
  iid      = 70 more i.i.d. draws from the same box (seed 29)            -> failure-rate bound uses the 100 i.i.d. draws only
  overload = 0.8 kg far-centre puck (outside the prior): 16 corners of (dx, dy, yaw, mu) + 10 interior
  falsify  = CMA-ES per placement (adversarial, not counted in the bound), candidates below margin 0.05 re-checked densely

Per segment we report: reached, admissible here (no U entry, every backup safe and in R, boundary ROA ok), success (ground truth),
failures, failures whose backup ends in R, silent failures (unsuccessful but the observable check passed).
Guarantee type: sampled.  Admission over the box = every library theta that reaches the segment is admissible there.

Expectations (to be checked, not assumed):
  - refusals at B0 near yaw -8 / dx -1.5 corners (left joint margin) and where the right arm cannot reach with clearance;
  - lid-unseating counterexamples in low-friction worlds (seen in the pilot: lid 5-7 mm after a push backup at mu x0.6);
  - overload worlds: clamp misses or slips caught by the boundary check / monitor; every failure should end in R.
Kill criterion for the rule as built: any failure whose backup does not end in R, or any nominal U entry, is a counterexample
and is reported with its world and time; it is not tuned away after the fact.
