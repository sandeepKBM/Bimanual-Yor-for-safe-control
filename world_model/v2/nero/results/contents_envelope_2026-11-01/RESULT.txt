Do the objects reach those ranges?  NO.  pan / pot (18 cm x 4 cm, 1.02 L) top out at ~1.0-1.2 kg of contents, below every
threshold: one arm always suffices.  Our sim Theta box (to 1.5 kg) is already beyond what the pot can physically hold with water-density
contents.  Tray with 2 mugs: 1.1 kg max, one arm.  Hidden contents change the action only for heavier loads: >= 4 mugs (2.2 kg),
a deeper pot (e.g. 20 x 10 cm, ~3 L), or a box of cans/books (the shelf task).
Caveat: the weld removes the grasp-wrench limit.  A one-arm carry by one side handle of a 0.34 m pot with 1.8 kg puts about
m g L ~ 3.6 Nm about the jaw axis on a parallel claw; the earlier one-claw pan runs (contents 0 / 0.2 / 0.4 kg) twisted
10-12 deg at 10 N and 1-2 deg at 40 N (results/contact_model_2026-11-01/claw40).  Under the weld this does not show, so the one-arm bands above are optimistic for real claws.
